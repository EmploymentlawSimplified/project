A New Start Begins With a Safe Place to Live

We provide exempt supported accommodation for vulnerable adults across Devon — combining safe, stable housing with structured key-worker support to help residents rebuild their lives.

What We Do

Housing With Support, Not Just a Roof

We go beyond providing accommodation. Every resident receives structured, person-centred support designed to break the cycle of homelessness and build lasting independence.

🏠

Supported Accommodation

Safe, high-quality HMO properties with intensive housing management, daily safety checks, and tenancy sustainment support to keep residents housed and stable.

🤝

1-on-1 Key-Working

Every resident is assigned a dedicated key-worker who provides structured case management, escorts to appointments, and advocacy across health, probation, and social services.

📈

Recovery & Life Skills

In-house workshops covering relapse prevention, budgeting, nutrition, employability skills, and peer support — building self-reliance and preparing residents for independent living.

Who We Support

Serving Devon's Most Vulnerable Adults

People Experiencing Homelessness

Rough sleepers and those in emergency temporary accommodation who need stable, supported housing to begin their recovery.

People in Substance Recovery

Individuals in active recovery who need a structured, substance-free environment with peer support and professional key-working.

Prison Leavers

People leaving custody who face homelessness and need accommodation linked to probation compliance and community reintegration.

Domestic Abuse Survivors

Individuals fleeing abusive situations who require safe, confidential accommodation with wraparound support services.
