About Us

Built to Serve, Not to Profit

The New Start Project Community CIC exists for one reason: to provide safe, high-quality supported housing for Devon's most vulnerable adults — and to reinvest every penny back into expanding that mission.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

Housing First, Always

We believe stable housing is the foundation everything else is built on — recovery, employment, health, and community reintegration all begin with a safe place to live.

Support, Not Just Shelter

Every resident receives structured key-worker support, escort services to appointments, and access to in-house workshops. We don't just house people — we help them rebuild.

Accountability Through Structure

As a Community Interest Company, 100% of our surplus is asset-locked and reinvested into expanding supported housing across Devon. No private extraction, full transparency.

Aligned With Local Need

Our model is built directly around Torbay Council's published Housing Needs Assessment 2025–2030, which identifies an urgent need for 4-bed HMOs with floating support for single vulnerable adults.

Our Structure

What Is a Community Interest Company?

A CIC is a special type of limited company designed for social enterprises that want to use their profits and assets for the public good.

🔒

Asset Lock

All assets and surpluses are permanently locked within the company. They can only be used to further our community objectives — never extracted for private gain.

📋

Community Interest Test

We must demonstrate that our activities benefit the community. The CIC Regulator oversees this through our annual CIC34 report, ensuring ongoing accountability.

🏛️

Regulated & Transparent

We file annual accounts with Companies House and submit reports to the CIC Regulator, providing a level of public transparency that private operators cannot match.
