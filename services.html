Our Services

Structured Support That Works

We deliver exempt supported accommodation with intensive housing management — combining safe housing with the structured, person-centred support that helps residents sustain tenancies and rebuild their lives.

Core Service

Supported Accommodation & Intensive Housing Management

Our properties provide more than a roof — they provide a structured environment where residents can stabilise and begin their recovery journey.

Safe, Quality HMO Properties

Fully compliant 4-bed properties in Torquay and Paignton, meeting all HMO licensing, fire safety, and health standards.

Daily Safety & Welfare Checks

Regular property inspections, welfare checks, and facility maintenance to ensure a safe and habitable environment at all times.

Tenancy Sustainment

Active support with rent management, room upkeep, neighbour relations, and compliance with house rules to prevent tenancy breakdown.

Move-On Planning

From day one, we work with residents towards sustainable independent living — whether that's a social tenancy, private rental, or other supported pathway.

Person-Centred Support

1-on-1 Key-Working, Escort & Advocacy

Every resident is matched with a dedicated key-worker who provides consistent, structured support throughout their placement.

📋

Individual Support Plans

Tailored support plans reviewed regularly, setting clear goals across housing, health, recovery, employment, and social reintegration.

🚗

Escort to Appointments

Physical accompaniment to probation, NHS, addiction recovery meetings, adult social care, and job centre appointments — reducing DNAs and maintaining compliance.

📢

Advocacy & Liaison

Acting as the resident's advocate across statutory services, ensuring their voice is heard and their needs are represented in multi-agency settings.

Building Independence

In-House Recovery & Life-Skills Workshops

Structured group sessions that build the practical skills and emotional resilience residents need for long-term independent living.

💪

Relapse Prevention

Evidence-based sessions helping residents identify triggers, develop coping strategies, and build a sustainable recovery framework.

💰

Budgeting & Financial Skills

Practical workshops on managing money, understanding bills, setting up bank accounts, and preparing for the financial responsibilities of independent living.

🍳

Cooking & Nutrition

Hands-on sessions teaching healthy meal preparation on a budget, promoting physical health and daily routine.

💼

Employability & CV Skills

Support with CV writing, interview preparation, job searching, and connecting with local employment services and training providers.

Our Model

Exempt Supported Accommodation

Our service is classified as exempt accommodation under housing benefit regulations — meaning residents receive Enhanced Housing Benefit that covers the full cost of their placement, including the intensive support element.

Housing With Support, Not Regulated Care

Our model provides housing management and support services — not personal care. This keeps the service outside CQC registration requirements while delivering meaningful, structured assistance.

No Cost to the Resident

Core rent and eligible service charges are covered by Enhanced Housing Benefit. Residents are only responsible for personal utilities (water, electric, food) — charged separately and transparently.
